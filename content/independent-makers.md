Shopping research for the November 2–13, 2026 Ireland itinerary. Researched October 15, 2026.

## Shopping preferences and planning notes

Prioritize independent Irish shops, working makers, community craft outlets, specialist booksellers, and local food shops. The aim is to find worthwhile objects and experiences connected to the places visited, rather than chains or generic souvenir stores.

Published hours below are regular schedules checked during research, not confirmed opening for the individual November travel dates. Small studios and shops with conflicting or seasonal information should be checked before diverting. Route fit is based on the saved itinerary; camper parking has not been verified for these shops.

Irish-designed and Irish-made are different claims. Where a shop carries mixed stock, ask who made a particular item and where it was produced. No shops have been contacted and no purchases or reservations have been made.

## First priorities

1. **Studio Donegal, Kilcar:** working handweaving mill and shop, potentially on the November 6 coastal route near Slieve League.
2. **Rosie's Pottery, Easkey:** pottery made and fired on-site, on the optional coastal R297 route through west Sligo.
3. **The Cat & The Moon, Sligo:** a shop whose stated policy is that everything is designed and made in Ireland.
4. **Leitrim Design House, Carrick-on-Shannon:** Irish-made craft in The Dock arts centre, on the November 3 route.
5. **Irish Design Shop, Dublin:** a strong concentration of work by Irish designer-makers, convenient for November 11–12.

**FOXFORD's mill shop is already built into the November 4 itinerary** alongside the confirmed 11:30 mill experience.

## Athlone, Roscommon and Carrick-on-Shannon — November 2–3 or 10

### Bastion Gallery — 6 Bastion Street, Athlone

- **What to buy:** Irish pottery, art, candles and jewellery, including its own **Ogham Treasure** jewellery. Especially relevant to the trip's interest in ancient Ireland.
- **Why it fits:** An independent craft shop in Athlone's Left Bank area, near Seán's Bar.
- **Trip fit:** **November 10 is the better prospect**, after returning the camper and travelling to Athlone. November 2 is a Monday.
- **Hours:** The website advertises Tuesday–Saturday, noon–5, but also contains old seasonal notices. **Confirm current opening before visiting.**
- **Sources:** [Discover Ireland shop information](https://www.discoverireland.ie/westmeath/the-bastion-gallery), [official contact page](https://bastiongallery.com/pages/contact-us)

### Claypipe Visitor Centre — Knockcroghery, County Roscommon

- **What to seek:** Traditional clay pipes made through a craft revived by Ethel Kelly, plus the history of a distinctive local industry. The county tourism office describes demonstrations.
- **Trip fit:** On the **N61 between Athlone and Roscommon**, if that is the transfer route used. Consider before camper pickup on November 3 or after return on November 10, subject to transport arrangements.
- **Hours/access:** **Call ahead.** Current November shop, demonstration and purchasing arrangements are unconfirmed. County tourism lists **+353 (0)90 6661923**.
- **Source:** [Visit Roscommon](https://www.visitroscommon.ie/claypipe-museum/)

### Leitrim Design House — The Dock, Carrick-on-Shannon

- **What to buy:** Irish-made ceramics, glass, jewellery, textiles, woodwork and prints, representing local and national makers.
- **Why it fits:** A craft and design outlet in the former courthouse arts centre, with a direct connection to Irish makers.
- **Address:** The Dock, St George's Terrace, Carrick-on-Shannon, N41 T2X2.
- **Trip fit:** **November 3**, during the Carrick stop. Shopping would consume part of the limited time remaining after the 14:00 camper pickup and handover.
- **Hours:** Published weekday opening **10:00–18:00**. This gives it a better chance than Boyle after the handover, though the overall evening route remains tight.
- **Sources:** [Official shop](https://leitrimdesignhouse.ie/), [The Dock's description](https://www.thedock.ie/visit/the-leitrim-design-house)

### Úna Bhán Craft Shop — King House grounds, Boyle

- **What to buy:** Regional pottery, knitted and felted accessories, turned wood, soaps, preserves, art, cards and books.
- **Why it fits:** A community-based outlet supporting makers from Boyle and the surrounding region.
- **Trip fit:** Your likely late **November 3** arrival may miss opening. A brief **09:00 November 4** visit could work only if it preserves the departure needed for the 11:30 Foxford appointment.
- **Hours:** Published **Monday–Saturday, 09:00–17:00**; Sunday opening is advertised for July and August only.
- **Sources:** [Craft shop](https://discoverboyle.ie/craft-shop/), [official opening hours](https://discoverboyle.ie/)

## Mayo — November 4–5

### FOXFORD mill shop — Foxford

- **What to buy:** Woven throws, blankets and scarves connected to the mill visit. Ask staff which particular pieces were woven there; the store also sells broader homewares.
- **Trip fit:** Already on the itinerary for **November 4**, with the **11:30 FOXFORD Experience confirmed for two people**. Allow browsing within the existing café/shop time rather than adding another drive.
- **Hours:** Published Wednesday shop opening **10:00–17:00**. The café has separate hours.
- **Sources:** [Mill experience](https://www.foxford.com/en-gb/pages/foxford-experience), [store hours](https://www.foxford.com/pages/store-locator)

### Marlene's Chocolate Haven — James Street, Westport

- **What to buy:** Chocolates made on the premises by Marlene, including flavours incorporating ingredients such as Achill sea salt, local honey, Mescan beer and Mayo spirits. Individual flavours depend on stock.
- **Trip fit:** **November 4**, during the Westport lunch and walk.
- **Hours:** The official site labels its Tuesday–Saturday **10:00–17:30** schedule as **summer hours**. **Confirm November Wednesday opening.**
- **Source:** [Maker's story, ingredients and hours](https://marleneschochaven.com/about/)

### Kelly's Butchers — 17 Main Street, Newport

- **What to buy:** Puddings, sausages and Irish meat for camper meals, plus local honey, condiments and other produce. The shop states that all its meat is Irish, some from its own farm.
- **Trip fit:** **November 4**, on the onward journey toward Nevin's. Particularly useful for buying breakfast supplies to enjoy during the trip.
- **Hours:** Published **Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00**; closed Sunday.
- **Source:** [Official shop details](https://kellysbutchers.com/contact-us/)

### Gift of Hands — behind the Old Irish Goat Centre, Mulranny

- **What to buy:** Rugs, cushions, textile pictures and soft toys made by local volunteers from **FOXFORD mill offcuts**, alongside work by other Mayo craftspeople.
- **Why it fits:** A direct connection to the mill visited earlier in the day; proceeds from Gift of Hands products support local community projects.
- **Trip fit:** Near the **November 4 Nevin's/Mulranny overnight area**. It still requires time beyond simply arriving at Nevin's.
- **Hours:** **Shop-specific November hours unconfirmed.** The tourism page's listed tourist-office hours should not be treated as the craft shop's opening hours.
- **Source:** [Mulranny community information](https://mulranny.ie/gift-of-hands/)

## West Sligo and Sligo town — November 5–6

### Rosie's Pottery and Crafts — Easkey

- **What to buy:** Rosemary McGowan's everyday bowls, mugs and serving pieces, made and fired **on-site**, with hand-painted and flower-impressed decoration. The shop also carries work by local makers.
- **Why it fits:** A particularly strong working-maker stop, rather than a general souvenir outlet.
- **Trip fit:** **November 5 if choosing the coastal R297 through Easkey.** Could combine pottery, the ruined seaside castle and a village stop. Exact fit depends on the onward route and chosen route from Ballina.
- **Hours:** **November opening needs checking before diverting.**
- **Source:** [Rosie's official studio website](https://rosiespotterystudio.com/)

### The Cat & The Moon — 4 Castle Street, Sligo

- **What to buy:** Martina Hamilton's coastal-inspired jewellery, ceramics, glass and textiles.
- **Why it fits:** Founded by jewellery designer Martina Hamilton, the shop states that **everything it sells is designed and made in Ireland**. One of the strongest choices for a substantial Irish craft gift.
- **Trip fit:** November 5 or 6 only if the schedule permits a Sligo town-centre stop. The Friday morning departure for Slieve League is already early; do not assume a leisurely shop visit fits before the cliffs.
- **Hours:** Published **Monday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00**.
- **Parking:** Separate town-centre camper parking needs arranging.
- **Source:** [Official shop background and hours](https://thecatandthemoon.ie/pages/about-us)

## Donegal — November 6

### Studio Donegal — The Glebe Mill, Kilcar

- **What to buy:** Handwoven throws, scarves, caps, garments and tweed, plus knitting yarn.
- **Why it fits:** A family-run working mill. Products bearing the **Studio Donegal label are handwoven and made in Kilcar**. Visitors are welcome to see the work being done.
- **Address:** Kilcar, County Donegal, **F94 EYF4**.
- **Trip fit:** Potentially on the coastal route between Killybegs and Slieve League, depending on the road chosen. **The number-one shopping priority from this research.** Allocate time deliberately while protecting departure from the cliffs by 14:00 and Ballyshannon arrival by 16:00.
- **Friday hours:** Published shop **09:00–17:30**; handweaving **09:00–16:00**. Confirm before travelling. The website has inconsistent weekend wording, which does not affect the planned Friday visit.
- **Contact:** +353 (0)74 9738194; info@studiodonegal.ie.
- **Source:** [Official mill and visiting information](https://studiodonegal.ie/)

## Galway — November 7–9

### My Shop… Granny Likes It — 29–31 Upper Abbeygate Street

- **What to buy:** Contemporary ceramics, prints, jewellery and homewares from Irish makers and designers.
- **Origin note:** Its own description distinguishes **Irish-made and Irish-designed** work. Ask about the particular piece's production if buying something made in Ireland is the priority.
- **Trip fit:** During the Galway stay. Sunday afternoon competes with TULCA and music; Monday morning is another possibility.
- **Hours:** Exact November Sunday opening requires confirmation; public listings differ.
- **Source:** [Official shop background](https://www.myshopgranny.com/service/about/)

### Charlie Byrne's Bookshop — The Cornstore, Middle Street

- **What to seek:** Irish folklore, local history, archaeology, poetry and contemporary Irish writers. Ask staff for books connected to places visited on the trip.
- **Trip fit:** **Monday, November 9 morning** offers more browsing time than the art-filled Sunday.
- **Hours:** Published **Sunday 11:00–17:00**; **Monday–Thursday 09:00–18:00**; Friday 09:00–20:00; Saturday 09:00–18:00.
- **Sources:** [Bookshop](https://charliebyrne.ie/), [official hours](https://charliebyrne.ie/about/opening-hours/)

### Thomas Dillon's Claddagh Gold — 1 Quay Street

- **What to buy:** A Claddagh ring from a long-established Galway jeweller.
- **Why visit:** A small Claddagh ring museum adds interest even if only browsing. Do not interpret marketing claims about “original” rings as meaning no other Galway maker produces authentic rings.
- **Trip fit:** Easy to combine with a Latin Quarter walk during the Galway stay.
- **Hours:** Confirm Sunday opening before planning around it.
- **Sources:** [Official shop](https://claddaghring.ie/), [museum description](https://www.visitgalway.ie/explore/places-of-interest/unusual-attractions/worlds-smallest-claddagh-ring/)

### Bell Book & Candle — Small Crane / Sea Road

- **What to buy:** Secondhand books, vinyl records and CDs. Ask about Irish artists and local releases.
- **Trip fit:** Convenient to the Westend venues already in the itinerary, especially the November 7 market area.
- **Hours:** Current opening needs checking.
- **Source:** [Galway's Westend business listing](https://galwayswestend.ie/business-category/shop/)

## Dublin — November 11–12

### Irish Design Shop — 41 Drury Street

- **What to buy:** Ceramics, glass, textiles, woodwork, prints and jewellery from Irish designer-makers.
- **Why it fits:** Founded by makers Clare Grennan and Laura Caffrey, with a strong connection to the people producing the work. The first Dublin craft-shopping priority.
- **Trip fit:** Either Dublin day; combine with nearby central shopping streets on foot from central Dublin.
- **Hours:** Current November opening should be checked before visiting.
- **Sources:** [Official location](https://irishdesignshop.com/pages/location), [craft background](https://2022.homofaber.com/en/visit/shops-irish-design-shop)

### STABLE of Ireland — 2 Westbury Mall, Balfe Street

- **What to buy:** Linen scarves, woven wraps, knitwear and household linens; a candidate for a substantial textile purchase.
- **Origin note:** The collection centres on Irish design and production. Discuss the individual product's materials and production in-store.
- **Trip fit:** November 11 or 12, conveniently combined with the Grafton Street area.
- **Hours:** Published **Monday–Saturday, 10:00–18:00**; closed Sundays and public holidays.
- **Sources:** [Official store details](https://www.stableofireland.com/pages/our-store), [company background](https://www.stableofireland.com/pages/about-us)

### Jam Art Factory — Crown Alley or Patrick Street

- **What to buy:** Prints and other work by Irish-based artists and illustrators, including contemporary Irish humour and street-art influences.
- **Why it fits:** Founded by Dublin brothers John and Mark to give artists a place to sell their work. A good option for an affordable piece with personality.
- **Locations:** 14/14A Crown Alley, Temple Bar; 64/65 Patrick Street, between Christ Church and St Patrick's Cathedral. Official pages vary slightly in unit numbering; use the shop's current directions.
- **Trip fit:** November 11–12; choose the branch that fits the day's sightseeing.
- **Hours:** Published **10:00–18:00 daily**.
- **Sources:** [Artists and shop story](https://jamartfactory.com/), [locations](https://jamartfactory.com/contact-us/)

### Sweny's — 1 Lincoln Place

- **What to buy:** The lemon soap associated with James Joyce's *Ulysses*, alongside a literary encounter in the preserved former pharmacy.
- **Trip fit:** Especially convenient to **central Dublin** on November 11–12.
- **Hours:** Published **11:00–18:00 daily**, with late Thursday readings advertised. Confirm the reading programme if interested on November 12.
- **Source:** [Official website](https://www.sweny.ie/)
