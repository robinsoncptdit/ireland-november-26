## Details to establish before choosing a ride

- the rider's recent riding frequency and comfort with English tack, posting trot and independent cantering.
- One adult rider travelling with a non-riding companion who wants to wait in the camper.
- Exact date, departure time and meeting point; total time on-site and any tide restrictions.
- Whether the ride includes a pace beyond walking and how riders are grouped or assessed.
- Current price, supplied helmet/boots, applicable rider requirements and cancellation/weather terms.
- Camper dimensions, access, turning space, parking and permission to remain during the ride.
