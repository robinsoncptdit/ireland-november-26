Research for the November 2–13, 2026 Ireland itinerary. Researched October 15, 2026.

## What this list is for

Everyday takeaway food: chicken fillet rolls, sausage rolls, spice bags, breakfast rolls and inexpensive chipper meals. Prioritize good local counters and takeaways over sit-down restaurant meals.

These are research recommendations, not personally tasted rankings. Strong recommendations are distinguished from practical backups and leads with incomplete menu information. Some deli counters use Centra or SPAR branding; recommendations refer to the specific branch, not the entire brand.

Prices are those displayed online during research unless explicitly dated otherwise. Delivery-app prices may differ from collection or counter prices. Regular opening hours are not confirmed opening for the individual November dates; hot-deli hours may be shorter than shop hours. Camper parking is unverified. No businesses have been contacted or orders placed.

## Strongest picks

- **Chicken fillet roll:** Declan & Donal's, Dublin; Sweeney Oil / SPAR at Headford Point is the most useful locally recommended Galway deli lead.
- **Sausage roll:** Magpie Bakery, Galway; The Creel Deli, Westport, is the strongest specific Mayo lead. Bread 41 is a more expensive bakery option convenient to central Dublin.
- **Spice bag:** Park Kitchen, Sligo, if the route and afternoon timing permit; Xi'an on Quay Street, Galway, or South Anne Street, Dublin, otherwise.

- **Evidence:** Current menu and value are better established than a claim that this is Athlone's best spice bag. Confirm opening for the intended evening.
- **Source:** [Official ordering menu](https://sohoasianstreetfood.ie/)

## Dublin — November 11–12

### Declan & Donal's, 34 Bolton Street — chicken fillet roll priority

- **Order:** Hot chicken roll with plain, spicy or Cajun chicken; choose fillings and sauce.
- **Official menu prices:**
  - Hot chicken roll, with cheese and four toppings: **€7**.
  - Breakfast roll: **€7.50**.
- **Why visit:** Independent reviewers specifically praise the bread, hot chicken and assembly. The strongest deliberate chicken-roll destination from this research.
- **Trip fit:** Lunch while exploring the north side on November 11 or 12. Requires a walk beyond the immediate area around central Dublin.
- **Hours:** Current service hours need confirming; plan as a daytime deli, not an evening takeaway.
- **Sources:** [Official menu](https://declananddonal.com/), [firsthand review](https://www.theliberty.ie/2024/02/27/declan-and-donal-the-best-deli-in-dublin/)

### Bread 41, 41 Pearse Street — sausage roll

- **Order:** Sausage roll from the bakery counter.
- **Dated price:** **€5.70 in the Irish Times, July 2025**. Confirm the current price.
- **Why visit:** A well-regarded bakery sausage roll convenient to central Dublin. This is a more expensive bakery option than a basic hot-deli roll.
- **Hours:** Official locations page publishes **07:00–15:00 daily**.
- **Trip fit:** November 12 breakfast or early lunch; November 11 also possible depending on arrival. Do not assume the November 13 airport departure leaves time for it.
- **Sources:** [Official location and hours](https://bread41.ie/locations/), [dated price and description](https://www.irishtimes.com/food/2025/07/19/irelands-bakery-boom-how-did-we-get-from-cream-slices-to-5-croissants/)
