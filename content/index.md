## Our plan and our maybes

Think of **Wanderlog as the Constitution**: it holds the adopted where, when and what. This wiki holds **unadopted amendments**—possibilities for bad weather, closures, low energy or spare time. The itinerary here is a public summary of that plan, not a separately edited replacement. Approving a wiki edit, liking an idea or booking something does not by itself update Wanderlog.

**Giscus and Hypothesis are the debating floor.** Discuss an amendment, suggest a revision or propose a new one. Supported changes can become wiki edits after review; they remain maybes until deliberately adopted into Wanderlog. Please link evidence for factual corrections and preserve earlier context when revising an idea.
