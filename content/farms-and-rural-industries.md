## What to ask when arranging a visit

Explain that two adults are interested in the practical work and livelihood behind the farm, including both growing and husbandry, and would value a farmer-led conversation. Ask:

- Can you accommodate two visitors on the proposed November date, and at what price or minimum charge?
- What will actually be happening on the farm then, and what can visitors see?
- Can the camper safely reach and park at the meeting point?
- Which parts of the business earn their keep, and which require the most winter labour?
- What would you start differently if you were beginning again today?
